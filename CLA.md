## How to accept

Confirm the checkbox in the pull request template:

> - [ ] I have read the [CLA](https://github.com/stratum-eng/stratum/blob/main/CLA.md) and I agree to it for this contribution.

Checking it, with the pull request submitted from your GitHub account, is your
acceptance of this agreement for that contribution and every later one you
submit, until this document changes. If a revised version is published, you will
be asked to accept it before your next contribution is merged.

If you would rather record acceptance some other way — a signed copy, or an
entity agreement executed by your employer — open a
[discussion](https://github.com/stratum-eng/stratum/discussions) and say so.
